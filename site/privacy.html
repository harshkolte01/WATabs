Privacy

Short stub for the public site. A full policy will ship with the production release (Phase 8).

What stays on your device

  • WhatsApp Web session data in per-account Electron partitions
  • Local labels, settings, download history metadata, optional PIN verifier
  • Application logs with redaction rules — no message bodies

What WATabs does not do

  • No WhatsApp DOM scraping or message reading by the shell
  • No cloud sync of sessions or chats
  • No session export or remote control
  • No advertising identity graph

App lock

Optional PIN lock is a local UI barrier. It is not full-disk encryption of WhatsApp session data.

Diagnostics

Support bundles are opt-in and redacted. You choose when to export them; they are not uploaded automatically.

Updates (Phase 7)

When auto-update exists, the app contacts GitHub Releases (or the configured feed) to check versions. That check does not include your chats or account contents.
